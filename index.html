Practice Areas

Workers' Compensation

Financial and medical benefits for employees injured or ill due to their job. Protects workers and employers, ensuring care and accountability.

Personal Injury

Compensation for those harmed by negligence. Covers medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and more.

Defense of the Uninsured Employer

Legal defense for employers facing claims without workers' compensation insurance. Navigates regulations and protects rights.

Subsequent Injuries Benefits Trust Fund

The Subsequent Injuries Benefits Trust Fund (SIBTF) provides additional compensation to workers who have a pre-existing disability and sustain a new work-related injury that together result in greater overall disability. It protects employers from full liability and encourages the hiring of workers with prior disabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cases does Moradi Law Group, APC handle?
We focus on workers’ compensation, personal injury, SIBTF, and defense of uninsured employers. Our goal is to protect your rights and guide you through the legal process with skill and dedication.
Do I have to pay upfront for legal services?
No. For most cases, including workers’ compensation and personal injury, we work on a contingency fee basis. That means you don’t pay us unless we successfully recover compensation on your behalf.
What should I do if I was injured at work?
Report your injury to your employer as soon as possible, seek medical attention, take pictures if applicable, obtain possible witness information, and keep records of your injury and treatment. Then, contact us so we can help you file a claim and ensure your rights are protected.
Why should I choose Moradi Law Group, APC?
We combine personalized attention with deep legal experience. Our team is committed to fighting for the best possible outcome, whether you are seeking compensation for an injury or defending against a claim as an employer.
How long do I have to file a claim?
Deadlines vary depending on the type of case. In workers’ compensation, you generally must notify your employer quickly, and personal injury claims have a statute of limitations that limits how long you have to file. Contact us right away to avoid missing important deadlines.
What if I don't speak English, do you have translators available?
Our office offers English, Farsi, and Spanish speaking personnel. Additionally, we have translators available for easy communication.
