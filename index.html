Why Choose Us

If We Don't Win,
You Don't Pay — Period.

When you're hurt, the last thing you should worry about is legal fees. At Moradi Law Group, APC, we operate on a pure contingency fee basis — meaning you owe us absolutely nothing unless we secure compensation for you. No hidden costs, no upfront retainers, no surprises.

Our firm has recovered over $500 million for injured clients across Los Angeles County and Southern California. With a 99% success rate across more than 15,000 personal injury cases, insurance companies know our name — and they know we fight.

When insurance companies see our name on a case, they take it seriously. Our track record of trial victories means they're far more likely to offer a fair settlement than risk facing us in court. That reputation is your leverage.
